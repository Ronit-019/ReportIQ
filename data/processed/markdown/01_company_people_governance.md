# **1. Company Overview** 

## **a. About Nexora Technologies** 

Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd. is a technology and digital transformation company founded in 2018 and headquartered in Bengaluru, India. 

The company provides technology engineering, cloud transformation, data and artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, digital experience and managed technology services to organizations across multiple industries. 

Nexora operates across India, the United Kingdom, Europe and North America. 

As of 01 April 2026, Nexora has approximately **1,180 employees and approved workforce members** across its operating locations. 

The company combines consulting, engineering and managed services capabilities to help customers modernize technology platforms, improve operational processes and build data-driven products. 

Nexora's operating philosophy is: 

**_Understand the problem. Protect the information. Build responsibly. Deliver measurable value. Learn continuously._**
