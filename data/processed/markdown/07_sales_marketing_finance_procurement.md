# **c. Commercial Principles** 

Nexora operates according to the following principles: 

## **I. Sustainable Growth** 

Revenue growth must be supported by healthy margins, delivery capability, and customer value. 

## **II. Accurate Commitments** 

Commercial commitments must reflect realistic delivery capability. 

## **III. Financial Discipline** 

Spending must have a legitimate business purpose and an accountable owner. 

## **IV. Customer Value** 

Pricing and commercial proposals should be connected to measurable customer outcomes. 

## **V. Segregation of Duties** 

No individual should control incompatible stages of a material financial transaction. 

## **VI. Transparency** 

Material commercial and financial risks must be visible to the appropriate decision-makers. 

## **VII. Responsible Procurement** 

Suppliers are evaluated not only on price but also on capability, reliability, security, quality, and total cost. 

## **VIII. Long-Term Relationships** 

Nexora seeks sustainable customer and supplier relationships rather than short-term transactions that create unacceptable operational risk.

# **Appendix J. Commercial Operating Principles** 

Nexora's Sales, Marketing, Finance, Procurement, and Commercial Operations teams operate according to the following principles: 

## **1. Revenue must be sustainable.** 

Growth should be supported by delivery capability, healthy economics, and customer value. 

## **2. The pipeline must represent reality.** 

Forecasts are useful only when opportunity stages, values, close dates, and risks are maintained accurately. 

## **3. A contract is an operational commitment.** 

Commercial terms affect delivery, finance, security, procurement, and customer success. 

## **4. Price is not the only measure of value.** 

Supplier and customer decisions should consider quality, risk, reliability, capability, and total cost. 

## **5. Financial controls protect the business.** 

Approval workflows and segregation of duties exist to protect Nexora and its employees. 

## **6. Customers should understand what they are buying.** 

Scope, deliverables, assumptions, dependencies, and acceptance criteria must be clear. 

## **7. Marketing claims must be supportable.** 

External statements should be accurate, approved, and appropriate for the intended audience. 

## **8. Suppliers are part of the operating ecosystem.** 

Third-party performance, security, resilience, and financial stability can affect Nexora's ability to serve customers. 

## **9. Exceptions require accountability.** 

When normal controls cannot be followed, the exception, reason, risk, mitigation, and approval must be visible. 

## **10. Commercial performance is a shared responsibility.** 

Sales, Finance, Delivery, Procurement, Marketing, Legal, Security, and Executive Leadership must work together to create durable business value.
