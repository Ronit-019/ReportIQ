# **Appendix K. Enterprise Operating Principles** 

Nexora's Legal, Corporate Operations, Business Continuity, and Knowledge functions operate according to the following principles: 

## **1. Protect the company's ability to operate.** 

Legal compliance, operational resilience, technology recovery, and knowledge continuity are connected responsibilities. 

## **2. Preserve important information.** 

Corporate records, contracts, decisions, and operational knowledge must remain accessible to authorized personnel. 

## **3. Know what matters most.** 

Continuity planning prioritizes critical people, processes, technology, information, customers, and suppliers. 

## **4. Prepare before disruption occurs.** 

A recovery plan written during a crisis is not an effective recovery plan.

# **5. Make ownership explicit.** 

Every critical process should have a responsible owner and an appropriate backup. 

# **6. Communicate with facts.** 

During legal matters, incidents, and crises, information should be accurate, controlled, and appropriately qualified. 

# **7. Treat knowledge as an operational asset.** 

Important knowledge must not depend entirely on one person's memory. 

# **8. Respect legal and contractual obligations.** 

Business urgency does not eliminate legal, regulatory, privacy, security, or contractual responsibilities. 

# **9. Test resilience.** 

Plans are useful only when they can be executed successfully under realistic conditions. 

# **10. Learn after every disruption.** 

Incidents, exercises, disputes, and operational failures should strengthen the organization's future resilience. 

# **11. Maintain a single source of truth.** 

Important policies, procedures, decisions, and operational records should reside in approved systems rather than scattered personal files. 

# **12. Continuity is everyone's responsibility.** 

Nexora's resilience depends on employees, technology teams, business functions, suppliers, leadership, and customers working together.
